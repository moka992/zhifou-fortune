Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot assets and source note
================================================

Card artwork
------------

The 78 card fronts and the Roses and Lilies card back bundled with this
application are scans of the original Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck, first
published in 1909. The artwork was created by Pamela Colman Smith under the
direction of Arthur Edward Waite.

Source collection:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck_(Roses_%26_Lilies)

Card back source:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waite%E2%80%93Smith_Tarot_Roses_and_Lilies_cropped.jpg

The source files are marked as public domain by Wikimedia Commons. They are
public domain in the United States because they were published before 1931,
and are identified by Commons as public domain in their country of origin.

Interpretive source
-------------------

The spread positions and the historical baseline for the card meanings are
based on Arthur Edward Waite's public-domain book "The Pictorial Key to the
Tarot". The Chinese text in this application is an original, concise modern
summary written for offline reference; it is not copied from a contemporary
commercial translation.

Public-domain text:
https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot

The application uses the ten drawn positions of Waite's Celtic method without
a separately preselected Significator. This avoids inferring a user's identity
from the historical gender-, age-, or appearance-based court-card convention.
